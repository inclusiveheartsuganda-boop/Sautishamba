PRODUCT + TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

Useful local advice, with an officer always in control.

SautiShamba is designed to help extension officers turn local conditions into clear recommendations for smallholder farmer groups, then prepare the advice in formats more farmers can use.

This is a front-end demo—not a live AI service.It uses fictional groups and illustrative values. No backend, live data, AI agent, MCP server, messaging connection or farmer delivery is active.
THE NEED

Problem and proposed solution

Problem

Officers need timely, local planting and market context. Information can be scattered, hard to verify, and difficult to communicate accessibly across languages and formats.

Solution concept

Bring group priorities and attributed conditions into one officer workspace; prepare a draft that can be checked and adapted before it goes anywhere.

For whom

Extension officers in Eastern Uganda are the primary users. Farmer groups are the intended recipients of approved advice—not users of this officer-desk prototype.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Approval is a hard boundary, not a suggestion

The AI must never directly message farmers.

A future system should reject delivery requests unless an authorized officer has approved the exact content and evidence. Editing after approval must invalidate that approval and require a fresh review. The present prototype has no delivery capability at all.

TRY THE DEMO

Setup and usage

Open index.html directly in a modern browser, or serve the repo root with python -m http.server 8000 and visit http://localhost:8000. There are no packages to install.

Search or filter groups, select one, inspect its sample conditions, then edit, reject or approve the draft. Explore each accessible-format tab. These actions stay in the front end; nothing is saved to a server or sent.

HONEST ABOUT THE GAPS

Limitations and next steps

Current limitations

Sample values are fictional and not agronomic advice. No login, backend, persistence, live feeds, AI, MCP, audit trail, or delivery. Accessible formats need real local-language production and community review.

Next improvements

Validate with local officers and farmers; integrate trusted, timestamped sources; add durable identity and approval audit; evaluate a grounded, non-autonomous drafting agent; and test language/accessibility with local partners.

Before any live launch

Review data rights, safety, security, source freshness, consent and delivery safeguards. Enforce officer approval on the server and prove that no model can bypass it.
